Who We Are

An Intellectual Platform for Tanzania's Future Leaders

CYPG bridges the gap between academia, institutions, and youth — preparing a generation to lead with competence and integrity.

CYPG is not simply a youth initiative. It is a structured platform designed to cultivate policy literacy, institutional awareness, and ethical leadership — moving youth engagement beyond reactive discourse and toward informed contribution to Tanzania's governance future.
